Recap

Wind becomes electricity in five steps

  1. 1Blades capture wind and spin the rotor and hub.
  2. 2The main shaft, supported by the main bearing, carries that rotation into the nacelle.
  3. 3The gearbox increases rotational speed for the generator.
  4. 4The generator converts mechanical rotation into electrical energy.
  5. 5The power converter and transformer condition and step up the electricity for transmission to shore.
